## Anti-Requirements for MaewSom
- **Do NOT rely purely on manual entry**: Manual entry fatigue is the biggest driver of churn for basic apps.
- **Do NOT auto-categorize silently if confidence is low**: Incorrect AI categories without a quick confirmation loop break trust.
- **Do NOT use a condescending tone when users are low on funds**: Empathy (Service Mind) is non-negotiable for Thai users.
- **Do NOT trap users in AI chat loops**: Build a flawless human bridge to support when NLP fails.
- **Do NOT bury core features in complex menus**: Avoid the "Super App" bloat (e.g., TrueMoney, SCB EASY). Keep the chat UI simple and focused.
