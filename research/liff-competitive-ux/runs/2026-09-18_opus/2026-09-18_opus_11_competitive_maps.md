# Competitive Positioning Maps

This document contains two competitive positioning maps placing the analyzed products across strategic axes, and explaining where MaewSom should position itself in the market.

## Map 1: Manual ←→ Automated × Dashboard ←→ Conversational

This map analyzes how products capture data (Manual vs. Automated) and how users interact with that data (Traditional Dashboard vs. Conversational UI).

**Quadrant 1: Automated & Conversational (The Sweet Spot for AI)**
- Cleo
- Ask Zopa
- **MaewSom (Target Position)**
- Bank of America (Erica)
- Copilot Money Assistant

**Quadrant 2: Automated & Dashboard (Traditional Aggregators)**
- Monarch Money
- YNAB (Plaid sync)
- SCB EASY
- K PLUS
- Rocket Money
- Origin
- Plum

**Quadrant 3: Manual & Dashboard (Traditional Trackers)**
- BooJot
- Money Diary
- รับจ่ายจด
- MeKinMeChai
- Wallet by BudgetBakers
- Lumpsum
- Spendee
- Piggipo

**Quadrant 4: Manual & Conversational (Niche/Emerging)**
- MAKE by KBank (Manual physical drag & drop, but chat-based review)
- Nabtang (Highly manual, simple UI, though not fully conversational)

### MaewSom's Positioning Strategy (Map 1)
**Position:** Automated & Conversational (Top Right).
**Rationale:** The Thai market is oversaturated with Manual/Dashboard trackers (BooJot, Money Diary) which suffer from high churn due to manual entry fatigue. Traditional banks (SCB, K PLUS) dominate Automated/Dashboard, but their interfaces are cluttered and rigid. By positioning MaewSom as Automated (via e-slip parsing and OCR) and Conversational (living natively inside LINE), we bypass app-fatigue and manual entry friction entirely. MaewSom intercepts the user in their natural environment (chat) and handles the data capture automatically.

## Map 2: Utility ←→ Relationship × Reactive ←→ Proactive

This map analyzes the emotional connection of the product (Pure Utility vs. Relationship/Persona-driven) and its behavior (Reactive/Wait-for-input vs. Proactive/Alert-driven).

**Quadrant 1: Relationship & Proactive (The Financial Partner)**
- Cleo
- Emma
- **MaewSom (Target Position)**
- Ask Zopa

**Quadrant 2: Utility & Proactive (The Silent Optimizer)**
- Rocket Money
- Copilot Money
- Plum
- ttb touch (Smart Insights)

**Quadrant 3: Utility & Reactive (The Traditional Tool)**
- SCB EASY
- K PLUS
- Bualuang mBanking
- MeKinMeChai
- Nabtang
- Lumpsum
- YNAB (mostly reactive to user input)

**Quadrant 4: Relationship & Reactive (The Friendly Ledger)**
- Piggipo (Mascot reacts to user input)
- MAKE by KBank
- MeowJot (Currently relies on user uploading slips, though mascot is friendly)

### MaewSom's Positioning Strategy (Map 2)
**Position:** Relationship & Proactive (Top Right).
**Rationale:** Most Thai banking apps are strictly Utility & Reactive (Quadrant 3); they do exactly what you ask, but nothing more, and lack emotional resonance. Piggipo and MeowJot proved that Thai users respond exceptionally well to Relationship (mascots/gamification). However, to truly differentiate, MaewSom must also be Proactive. It shouldn't just wait for a slip; it should proactively send a LINE message on Friday afternoon saying, "Hey, you've been doing great this week, you have 1,000 THB left in your fun budget for the weekend!" This creates a trusted "financial best friend" dynamic that drives long-term retention.
