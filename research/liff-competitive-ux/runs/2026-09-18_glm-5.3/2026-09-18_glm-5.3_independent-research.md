## Executive Summary

Based on research across 50+ products in the personal finance ecosystem, the report argues for a hybrid interaction model combining conversational AI with traditional dashboards, with separation between casual and serious financial contexts.

Main thesis:

- LINE-native lightweight interaction for frequent tasks.
- LIFF for complex financial workflows.
- AI transaction ingestion.
- Human-in-the-loop confirmation.
- Relationship progression with Serious Mode boundaries.

Primary proposed opportunity:

> LINE-native AI transaction ingestion + human verification + relationship progression.

## UX Patterns Proposed by GLM 5.3

### 1. Confidence-Based Automation
AI categorizes automatically and routes uncertain transactions to review.

Applicability: High.

### 2. Serious Mode Transition
Tone and visual intensity reduce for payments, warnings, reconciliation and other high-stakes states.

Applicability: High.

### 3. Conversational Ingestion
Users send images, voice or text instead of opening manual forms.

Applicability: High.

### 4. Review Inbox
Central queue for unresolved/uncertain items.

Applicability: High.

### 5. Progressive Disclosure
Expose complexity gradually.

Applicability: High.

### 6. Data Provenance
Show data source and verification state.

Applicability: High.

### 7. Relationship Progression
System evolves with user history and interaction.

Applicability: Medium.

### 8. Contextual Notifications
Trigger notifications based on user context.

Applicability: High.

### 9. Multi-Modal Input
Image / voice / text transaction capture.

Applicability: High.

### 10. Error Recovery with Learning
Corrections should improve future interpretation.

Applicability: Medium.

### 11. Household Finance Management
Shared finance patterns inspired by Honeydue / Spendee.

Applicability: Medium.

### 12. Financial Wellness Score
Simplified health indicator.

Applicability: Medium.
